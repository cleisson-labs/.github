# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews and other project conversations. Critique the change, explain technical disagreements and give others space to respond.

Harassment, threats, unwanted contact, discrimination, and sharing someone's private information are grounds for moderation. Maintainers may remove content, close or lock discussions, and restrict participation to keep the project welcoming.

Contact the maintainer privately through the contact options on [Cleisson's profile](https://github.com/cleissonom). Include a link to the relevant interaction and the outcome you need. Reports are handled in confidence to the extent needed for review and enforcement.
